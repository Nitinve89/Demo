# 30-Day Content Calendar — Bodybuilding (@nitinverma.fit)

**Goal:** Grow reach & followers from 250 → 1,000+ through saves- and shares-focused Reels.
**Cadence:** 4 Reels/week (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat suggested). Adjust to your schedule — consistency beats perfection.
**Every Reel:** on-screen hook (first 2s) • 8–15 sec • caption CTA • 3–5 niche hashtags • repost to Shorts + TikTok.

---

## Content type key
- 🟢 **SAVE** = save-bait (form, routines, tips) → drives reach
- 🔵 **SHARE** = share-bait (myths, humor, relatable) → drives reach
- 🟡 **TRUST** = physique/progress → drives follows
- 🟠 **ENGAGE** = question/comment-bait → drives algorithm signals

## Weekly rhythm (do these every week)
- **Before posting:** spend 10 min engaging with bodybuilding accounts in your niche.
- **First hour after posting:** reply to every single comment. This is the biggest free reach boost.
- **Stories:** 2–4 story slides/day (behind the scenes, meals, poll/question sticker). Keeps you visible.
- **End of week:** check Insights → note your best Reel → make more like it next week.
