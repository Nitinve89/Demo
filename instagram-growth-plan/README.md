# Instagram Growth Plan — @nitinverma.fit

Bodybuilding content strategy to grow reach and followers (starting at ~250 followers).

## Files
- **[30-day-content-calendar.md](./30-day-content-calendar.md)** — Day-by-day 30-day posting plan (4 Reels/week), profile setup, and a tracking table.
- **[15-reel-scripts.md](./15-reel-scripts.md)** — 15 ready-to-film Reel scripts with hooks, shot lists, captions, CTAs, hashtag sets, and a filming checklist.

## The core strategy in one line
Post short (8–15s) Reels with a strong on-screen hook in the first 2 seconds, ask for **saves** in every caption, reply to every comment in the first hour, and repost to YouTube Shorts + TikTok.

## Start here
1. Update your bio and profile (see the calendar).
2. Film Week 1's 4 Reels using the scripts.
3. Post consistently for 30 days, then double down on whatever topic got the most saves/shares.
