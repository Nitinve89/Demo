# 15 Ready-to-Film Reel Scripts — Bodybuilding (@nitinverma.fit)

**How to use this:**
- The **HOOK** is on-screen text for the first 2 seconds. Say it out loud too if you can.
- Keep each Reel **8–15 seconds**. Short = higher completion = more reach.
- End every caption with the **CTA** (call to action). This is what drives saves/shares.
- Use **3–5 niche hashtags** (list at the bottom). Don't use #fitness or #gym — too crowded.
- Reply to EVERY comment in the first hour.

## 1. Wider Back (Form / Save-bait)

**Caption:** Your back won't grow if you only chase weight. Focus on the stretch and the squeeze. Which one's your favorite?
**CTA:** Save this for your next back day 💾
